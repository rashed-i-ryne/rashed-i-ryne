## 🚀 About Me

- 🔭 **My Background:** I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) student at United International University, bringing a rigorous, analytical problem-solving foundation to my work as a Full-Stack MERN Developer.
- 🌱 **My Approach:** I specialize in bridging the gap between robust backend architecture and seamless frontend interfaces, heavily leveraging an engineering mindset and AI-assisted coding practices to ship reliable, high-performance applications.
- 🎨 **Passionate About:** The fine details of development. I love writing clean, efficient code—whether that means structuring secure databases or fine-tuning fluid CSS animations (such as perfectly timing a background transition from dark to light during sunrise, holding a vibrant light blue sky at high noon, and fading smoothly from light to dark at sunset, keeping the canvas entirely focused on the sky without any ground elements).
- 💡 **Client Focus:** My ultimate goal is to turn complex requirements into polished, user-friendly realities that drive real business value through clear communication and precise execution.
